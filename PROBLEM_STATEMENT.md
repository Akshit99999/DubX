# Regional Content Dubbing

**Category:** Media • Localisation  
**Event:** CODE कलेश 2026 — One Night Hackathon  
**Envelope:** 03 of 89  

---

## 1. The Problem
> **"Creators reach one language audience and stop. Dubbing is expensive, so regional viewers get very little good content."**

---

## 2. Your Brief
Build a pipeline that dubs video into regional languages keeping the speaker's voice character, with subtitles generated alongside.

---

## 3. What Your Team Must Prepare

### 1. Research the Problem
- **Who exactly has it:** Target audience/creators affected.
- **What it costs them today:** Current costs of translation, dubbing, voice actors, and studio time.
- **Who is already solving it and where they fall short:** Existing tools/solutions and their limitations.
- **How large the market is:** TAM / SAM / SOM for regional dubbing and media localization.
- **One real number that proves the problem:** Concrete data point or statistic demonstrating the scale/pain point.

### 2. Build the Business Plan
- **What you will sell:** Offering / product / API / service.
- **Who pays for it:** Customer personas (creators, media companies, edtech, platforms, etc.).
- **How much they pay:** Pricing model (pay-per-minute, subscription, tier-based, etc.).
- **Why they would switch to you:** Competitive advantage, cost savings, turnaround time, voice fidelity.

### 3. Automate One Step with AI
- Pick the step AI should take over.
- Build anything that proves it works: UI/UX screens, a website, an app, a working AI demo, or an automation workflow.
- Show the **before** and the **after**.

### 4. Make the Presentation
- **Minimum 5 slides:**
  1. Problem
  2. Your Solution
  3. What the AI Automates
  4. The Demo
  5. The Impact

### 5. Pitch It
- **Duration:** Two minutes
- **Structure:** Problem $\rightarrow$ Business $\rightarrow$ What you automated $\rightarrow$ Live demo $\rightarrow$ The impact
